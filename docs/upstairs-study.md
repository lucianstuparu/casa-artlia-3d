## Real scale and explicit assumptions

Adults have 160–188 cm reference standing statures and independently sized 50–58 cm breadths. Seated pose height is derived from sitting anatomy, not stretched to standing height. Parent seat height is 44 cm, nominal seat frontage 60 cm. Child statures are illustrative 90–138 cm; child stools are 30 cm high. People have occupied-body collision proxies matching their rendered bounds.

The four inset furnishing rectangles total **93.249 m²**. Each is smaller than its room's printed area. They constrain this spatial illustration without altering walls or resolving the room shape discrepancy. All occupied bodies and major furniture meshes stay within these envelopes, including the shallower house roof overhang. Rectangles do not establish measured clearances or prove that their exact shapes can be transferred to the real rooms.

At 60 visitors, printed gross activity area is about **1.60 m² per visitor**, before deducting furniture and staff. The playhouse deck is not counted as extra room area. Corridors, toilet, service rooms and exterior landing receive no overflow seats or activity populations. The local route stripes are study targets, not accessibility certificates. Door swings still matter; office doors are closed for the activity-room navigation checks.

The model tests body non-overlap and one-person movement, not simultaneous evacuation, moving children, bags, pushchairs, acoustics or operational supervision. Twenty or 24 families provide useful lower-density comparisons, not separately verified capacities. The30-per-day target alone does not determine simultaneous demand; visit duration and arrival patterns would be needed to estimate it.

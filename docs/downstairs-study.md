## What fits in this arrangement

Five waiting adults plus one operator fit tightly in two short files: two at check-in and three at coffee. Six waiting adults plus the operator can physically stand without body overlap, but the sixth intrudes into the entrance-turn target. This is a comparison of the modelled arrangement, not proof that every possible six-person layout is impossible.

The public-side strip is modelled with a 90 cm planning target, outside the retained stair rail; actual drawn bodies leave approximately 97 cm from the chosen x135cm planning boundary. The entrance-turn study uses a 90 cm diameter centred at x277,z45. For five people, the nearest actual body is about 53.9 cm from that centre; with six it is about 35.5 cm, inside its 45 cm radius. These are local spatial indicators, not a verified continuous accessible route or an ERP occupancy calculation.

People are representative adults with actual mesh heights 162–188 cm and independently normalized outer breadths 50–58 cm and depths 34–42 cm. The operator is 176 cm tall. They are not scaled down as the waiting count increases. Soft waiting cells are 60×60 cm; they illustrate close queue spacing rather than comfort allowances. Body-sized collision proxies match the rendered figures.

The stature range was checked against [CDC's published adult anthropometric table](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/222379/cdc_222379_DS1.pdf). Breadths, poses, clothing and individual combinations remain illustrative assumptions. There is no stroller, luggage, accompanying-child or whole-family allowance.

## Dual counter

A transverse counter occupies the rear of the usable entrance area, in front of the existing under-landing alcove:

- Left: nominal 60 cm-wide registration surface at 78 cm high, lifting to provide staff access.
- Right: nominal 102 cm-wide coffee cabinet at 95 cm worktop height.
- Both: 55 cm deep, with an approximately 85 cm staff floor aisle behind.
- Equipment: compact one-group espresso machine, grinder, shared admission/payment terminal, rinser, takeaway pickup cups, under-counter fridge and shallow cup shelf.
- One operator alternates between registration and coffee; this is not two simultaneous staffed service stations.

The existing alcove ceiling is 213 cm at its centre, with 211.5 cm clear underside in the model. It was not raised or removed. The registration surface lifts so a staff member can enter the rear area before the rush. Services, equipment maintenance clearances, hygiene workflow, dimensions and staff-access details remain proposals requiring site development.

## Platform use

Waiting customers must move for GTLE boarding and wheelchair manoeuvring. The “clear for platform use” scenario removes the waiting figures, retains the operator, and shows a 150 cm study ring and the lower-station footprint. It does not move or replace the existing lift; its original controls remain available. This study does not certify an accessible boarding layout.

## Review controls

Choose five, six or no waiting customers. “Plan and footprints” gives a true overhead orthographic view; “3D” shows the crowded entrance in context. Space markings are optional. “Staff access” lifts the registration surface.

The study temporarily hides the alcove ceiling/overhead landing and clips foreground facade surfaces in the cutaway so the downstairs arrangement can be examined. Those original surfaces return when walking or leaving the downstairs study. Their geometry is unchanged. First-person walking includes the downstairs people as obstacles; upstairs figures keep their previous illustrative behaviour.

## Verification

The dedicated downstairs test measures actual person bounds and floor level, checks counter/body non-overlap and alcove headroom, exercises the five/six comparison, traverses the busy entrance in both directions, tests the open/closed registration hatch and compares actual upstairs furniture/people meshes against v1.0.1. It also checks language changes and phone/short-screen layouts. The original variant stair/navigation and 48-state layout /36-state language checks pass; entrance animation is checked with the queue cleared.

Assumptions V1-A03–A07 record the revised counter, adult scale, crowding, render cutaway and platform-use limitations. Base assumptions remain unchanged.

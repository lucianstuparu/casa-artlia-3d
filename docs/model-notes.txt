Walking controls
- Visite, then Explorer en glissant.
- Hold either mouse button and drag to look around.
- Arrows, WASD or ZQSD move relative to the viewing direction; Shift moves faster.
- E or a brief left click operates a nearby door under the crosshair.
- Escape pauses and releases the mouse. Quitter restores the prior building/plan view.
- Regard > Souris capturée · FPS enables optional unrestricted mouse capture. Rejected, ignored or unresolved capture requests return to drag mode after 900 ms.
- Touch devices: drag to look, directional buttons to move, Porte to operate a targeted door.

# Continuous street façade — C05

The user confirmed that Ruelle des Jardins has one continuous wall at the entrance/bookshop junction, and that the grey entrance edge is painted on the dividing masonry. The exterior photo [20260419_160227.jpg](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UH6wZe1FLSu1YiI_cYSmNdk6nUUyWJVE/view) shows this shared street plane.

Both phases now render the adjacent street walls on the original entrance exterior plane, z=-4 cm. The previous context-wall face was about 24.5 cm forward of it. The entrance arch keeps its original profile and aperture, but its 20 cm projecting extrusion is replaced by a 0.08 cm render layer representing paint. The original telescopic door, glazing and animation remain in place.

The street-facing slab edges are inset by 0.12 cm for rendering, and masonry continues over the floor junctions. This removes false horizontal ledges. A narrow façade corner closes the original side-wall/front-wall join.

A17 distinguishes the user-confirmed coplanarity and painted finish from assumed backing thickness and small rendering offsets. The original v10 file is unchanged. Canonical ERP room polygons, wall traces, levels, PMR, landing recess, stairs and GTLE coordinates are unchanged; C05 is an explicit render registration.

Running-browser verification covers close oblique views and the full street elevation in both phases, plus 25 ray samples per phase across the masonry and floor junctions. Existing stairs, wall mounts, door/lift animation, layout, languages, PMR and landing-recess checks also passed.
